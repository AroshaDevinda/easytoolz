Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF file uploaded to any server?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using PDF.js. Your file never leaves your device.

What is the maximum file size?

There is no hard limit — it depends on your device's memory. Most PDFs up to 200MB work fine.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

Password-protected PDFs are not supported. Please remove the password first.
