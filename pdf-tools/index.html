Frequently Asked Questions

Are my confidential PDFs safe?

Yes. EasyToolz uses local browser processing for most of our PDF tools. This means your PDF file is processed entirely by your device's web browser and is never uploaded to our servers. Look for the "Privacy-first" notice on the tool page.

Is there a file size limit?

Because the processing happens in your browser, the limit depends on your device's memory. Most modern computers and phones can handle PDFs up to several hundred megabytes without any issues.

Do you add a watermark to my merged or compressed PDFs?

No, our utilities are completely free and will never add unexpected watermarks or branding to your final PDF documents.
