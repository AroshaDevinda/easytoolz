About EasyToolz

Free online tools for everyday tasks — simple, fast, and no sign-up needed.

What is EasyToolz?

EasyToolz.click is a collection of free, browser-based tools designed to make everyday tasks easier. Whether you need to compress an image, calculate your BMI, generate a QR code, or set a countdown timer — we have a tool for it.

All our tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Your privacy is 100% protected.

Our Mission

We believe that useful tools should be free, fast, and accessible to everyone. No paywalls, no accounts, no tracking. Just open the tool and get to work.

What We Offer

  • 27+ free online tools across 6 categories
  • Image tools: compress, resize, convert
  • Calculators: BMI, EMI, percentage, tip, discount
  • Text tools: word counter, case converter, lorem ipsum
  • Developer tools: JSON formatter, URL encoder, color picker
  • Daily tools: stopwatch, countdown timer, to-do list, notepad
  • Utilities: QR generator, password generator, unit & currency converter

Privacy First

All computations happen in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any data you enter into our tools. Your files and information never leave your device.
