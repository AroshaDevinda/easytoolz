Frequently Asked Questions

Are these image tools really free?

Yes, all the image tools provided by EasyToolz are 100% free to use. We don't hide features behind paywalls or add surprise watermarks to your downloads.

Do I need to install any software?

No, our tools operate entirely within your modern web browser. There are no downloads or installations required, meaning you can access them from any device safely.

Are my images uploaded to your servers?

Most of our image tools, including the compressor and resizer, process files locally in your browser. This means your private photos never leave your device. If a specific tool requires server-side processing, it will be explicitly stated on that tool's page.
