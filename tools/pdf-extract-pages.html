How to Extract Specific Pages from a PDF Online for Free

Often you only need a single receipt from a 50-page financial statement, one chapter from a textbook, or an individual schematic from an engineering spec sheet. EasyToolz Extract PDF Pages makes cherry-picking pages seamless and instantaneous.

  1. Upload your PDF: Drag and drop your file into the tool above or select it from your device storage.
  2. Choose pages to keep: Click page numbers in the visual grid, or type ranges into the input field (e.g. 1-4, 7, 11-15).
  3. Download your extracted PDF: Click "Extract Pages & Download PDF" to save your custom curated document.

Why Use EasyToolz for PDF Extraction?

  • Interactive Visual Selector: See all pages at a glance and click to toggle selection without typing if you prefer.
  • Safe for Confidential Files: Everything is parsed in your device's browser memory via WebAssembly. Nothing is ever transmitted to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I select which pages to extract?

You can either click individual page buttons in the interactive visual grid or type page ranges in the text box (such as 1-3, 5, 8-10).

Will my original PDF file be modified or damaged?

No. Your original file remains untouched on your device. The tool simply generates a new PDF file containing your selected pages.

Are my sensitive PDF files uploaded to any server?

Never. All extraction is computed directly inside your browser memory using PDF-lib. Nothing is sent to any server.

Will extracted pages retain their original visual quality?

Yes, all embedded graphics, fonts, formatting, and high-resolution images are duplicated losslessly.
