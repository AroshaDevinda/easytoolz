Versatile Online Countdown Timer for Study, Workouts & Cooking

Staying productive throughout the day requires disciplined time blocking. Whether you're practicing the Pomodoro technique for focused studying, timing high-intensity interval training (HIIT) circuits, or baking a recipe, EasyToolz Countdown Timer offers a clean, distraction-free display.

  1. Set hours, minutes & seconds: Click any of the quick preset buttons (like 25-minute Pomodoro or 5-minute break) or enter custom hours and minutes.
  2. Start the clock: Hit "Start". The digits tick down with second-by-second accuracy.
  3. Audio notification: An audible tone chimes when the timer hits zero so you never miss an alert even with your browser minimized.

Popular Ways to Use the Countdown Timer

  • Pomodoro Technique: 25 minutes of deep focused work followed by a 5-minute mental recharge.
  • Classrooms & Presentations: Display the large fullscreen timer on a projector for student quizzes, debates, or timed speech presentations.
  • Kitchen & Cooking: Keep track of boiling pasta, brewing coffee, or baking pastries.
  • Fitness & Planks: Measure rest periods between lifting sets or timed yoga holds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the timer sound an alarm when time is up?

Yes! When the countdown reaches zero, an audio chime plays to alert you even if you are working in another tab or across the room.

Can I pause and resume the countdown?

Yes, simply click the Pause button to freeze the timer and Resume whenever you're ready.

Does this work on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes, the timer features huge, responsive typography optimized for iPhones, Android phones, iPads, and large desktop monitors.

Is there an internet connection required while counting down?

Once the page loads, the timer runs purely via local JavaScript and Web Audio API with zero external network calls.
