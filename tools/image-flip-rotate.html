Correct Photo Orientation & Mirror Images Instantly

Mobile cameras and digital photo scanners often record incorrect EXIF orientation metadata, resulting in sideways or upside-down images when shared on social media or loaded onto web pages. Our free Image Flip & Rotate utility allows you to permanently fix photo orientation and mirror graphics with zero cloud uploads.

Flipping vs. Rotating: When to Use Which

  • Rotate 90° Left or Right: Used when a portrait smartphone photo opens in landscape orientation, or vice versa.
  • Rotate 180°: Fixes completely inverted or upside-down document scans in a single click.
  • Flip Horizontal (Mirror): Restores front-facing selfie shots to how you naturally look in a mirror, or flips graphic icons to point in the opposite direction.
  • Flip Vertical: Creates water-reflection effects or corrects inverted overhead camera captures.

Full Privacy with In-Browser Canvas Processing

Unlike online services that require uploading your personal family pictures or documents to remote servers, EasyToolz applies all matrix transformations using your device's native graphics acceleration via HTML5 Canvas. Your photos never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does rotating an image decrease its sharpness?

No. 90-degree and 180-degree rotations simply map pixel coordinates to their new orthogonal positions, ensuring full fidelity and sharpness.

What image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and GIF images, and download your transformed result in your preferred format (JPEG, PNG, or WebP).

Why do my smartphone photos sometimes appear sideways?

Some devices store orientation as EXIF tags rather than physically rotating the pixels. Our tool bakes the rotation permanently into the image pixels so it displays correctly everywhere.

Can I use this tool on my iPhone or Android device?

Yes. EasyToolz is fully responsive and supports touch interactions and direct camera library imports on all modern smartphones.
