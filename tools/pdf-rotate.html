How to Permanently Rotate PDF Pages Online for Free

Scanned documents often come out upside-down or sideways depending on how the feeder fed the paper. EasyToolz PDF Rotate allows you to rectify page angles in seconds and permanently save the corrected orientation.

  1. Add your PDF document: Drag and drop your file into the upload box above or choose it from your phone or computer.
  2. Select rotation angle: Choose 90° clockwise, 180° (upside down), or 90° counter-clockwise.
  3. Target specific pages: Rotate every page at once, or restrict rotation to odd, even, or specific page numbers (e.g. 2, 4, 7-9).
  4. Download your rotated PDF: Click "Rotate & Download PDF". The resulting file saves the corrected orientation permanently so any recipient sees it properly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does rotating a PDF in this tool save permanently?

Yes! Unlike a basic PDF viewer that only rotates for temporary viewing, EasyToolz updates the internal rotation dictionary of the PDF file so it opens right-side up on any device, app, or printer.

Can I rotate only odd pages or only even pages?

Yes! You can choose "All pages", "Odd pages only", "Even pages only", or enter custom page numbers like "1, 3-5, 7".

Are my sensitive documents uploaded to a remote server?

No. Everything executes locally on your device via PDF-lib in JavaScript. Your files are completely safe and confidential.

Will rotating degrade the text or image quality of my PDF?

Not at all. The underlying text streams, vector paths, and embedded images remain bit-for-bit unchanged.
