How to Calculate Tips and Split the Check Stress-Free

Dining out with a group should be about good conversation and great food, not awkward mental math at the end of the meal. EasyToolz Tip Calculator calculates gratuity in seconds and divides the tab equally among every guest.

  1. Enter your bill: Type the total food and drink subtotal from your receipt.

  3. Split evenly: Enter the number of people sharing the tab. The tool computes the individual contribution for each friend instantly.

Tipping Etiquette Guidelines

  • 15%: Standard baseline for polite, satisfactory table service.
  • 18% - 20%: Recommended industry standard for attentive, friendly dining service.
  • 22% - 25%: Exceptional service, large party accommodations, or fine dining experiences.
  • Food Delivery & Bartending: $3-$5 minimum or 15%-20% on food orders; $1-$2 per cocktail or beer at bars.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard tipping percentage at restaurants?

In the United States and Canada, standard restaurant tipping ranges from 15% for adequate service to 18%-20% for good service, and 22%-25% for exceptional dining.

How do you calculate tip and split per person?

Tip Amount = Bill × (Tip % / 100). Total Bill = Bill + Tip. Amount Per Person = Total Bill / Number of People.

Should tips be calculated before or after sales tax?

Etiquette experts recommend tipping on the pre-tax food and beverage subtotal, though tipping on the total after tax is also common.

What if service was unsatisfactory?

If service was sub-par, 10% is customary, accompanied by polite, constructive feedback to the manager.
