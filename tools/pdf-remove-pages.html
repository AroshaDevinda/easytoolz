How to Delete Pages from a PDF Document Free Online

Scanned documents frequently contain accidental blank pages, duplicate scans, or outdated annexes. EasyToolz Remove PDF Pages lets you strip out unnecessary or confidential pages in seconds without buying Adobe Acrobat Pro.

  1. Upload your document: Drop your PDF into the upload area above or select it from your computer or phone.
  2. Select pages to delete: Click on any page button in the visual selector grid to mark it red for removal, or type numbers directly (e.g. 2, 5, 8-10).
  3. Save your updated PDF: Click "Remove Pages & Download PDF" to retrieve your streamlined, clean document instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete unwanted pages from my PDF?

Click on the page numbers you want to delete in the interactive grid (they turn red), or type numbers like "1, 4, 7-9", then click "Remove Pages & Download".

Will removing pages affect the formatting of the rest of the document?

No. The remaining pages retain their exact layout, vector lines, text layers, and embedded imagery intact.

Are my PDF documents uploaded to an external server?

Never. All page removal logic runs locally inside your browser using PDF-lib WebAssembly. Nothing is ever transmitted to a server.

Can I delete multiple pages at once?

Yes! You can delete individual pages, non-consecutive pages, or continuous page ranges (e.g. 5-10) in a single click.
