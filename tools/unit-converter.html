How to Convert Units Online

Whether you're a student working on a physics assignment, a cook converting a recipe from cups to milliliters, a traveler switching between miles and kilometers, or an engineer converting between metric and imperial measurements — our free Unit Converter makes it instant.

  1. Select a category: Choose from Length, Weight, Temperature, Speed, Volume, Area, or Time.
  2. Pick your units: Select the "From" unit and the "To" unit from the dropdown menus.
  3. Enter a value: Type the number you want to convert. The result updates in real-time as you type.

Supported Unit Categories

  • Length: Millimeters, Centimeters, Meters, Kilometers, Inches, Feet, Yards, Miles
  • Weight: Milligrams, Grams, Kilograms, Ounces, Pounds
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin (uses proper formulas, not simple ratios)
  • Speed: m/s, km/h, mph, Knots
  • Volume: Milliliters, Liters, US Gallons, US Cups, Tablespoons, Teaspoons
  • Area: mm², cm², m², km², in², ft², Acres, Hectares
  • Time: Seconds, Minutes, Hours, Days, Weeks

Frequently Asked Questions

What units can I convert?

Our converter supports 7 categories with 40+ units: Length, Weight, Temperature, Speed, Volume, Area, and Time — covering the most commonly needed conversions.

How accurate is this converter?

We use precise scientific conversion factors. Temperature conversions use correct formulas (°C to °F = C × 9/5 + 32), not approximations. Results show up to 6 significant digits.

Does it work offline?

Yes! Once the page loads, the converter works entirely in your browser without an internet connection. Bookmark it for quick access.
