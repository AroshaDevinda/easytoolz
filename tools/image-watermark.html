How to Watermark Images Online in 3 Steps

Protecting your photography, digital art, product images, and social media content from unauthorized re-use has never been simpler. With EasyToolz Image Watermark, you can stamp copyright notices, company names, or website URLs directly onto your graphics in seconds.

  1. Upload your image: Drag and drop your JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo into the workspace above or click to select from your device.
  2. Customize watermark settings: Type your custom text, adjust font size, pick any brand color, configure transparency/opacity, and position the stamp in corners, center, or as a repeated tile pattern.
  3. Download your watermarked photo: Choose JPEG, PNG, or WebP format and hit Download to save your secured graphic instantly.

Why Watermarking is Essential for Creators & Businesses

  • Prevent Content Theft: Dissuade scrapers, competitors, and unauthorized accounts from reposting your original photography or designs.
  • Boost Brand Recognition: When your graphics go viral on Reddit, Instagram, Twitter/X, or Pinterest, your watermark directs viewers right back to your brand.
  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: Most online watermarking services upload your private images to third-party cloud servers. EasyToolz executes entirely in your browser memory — nothing is ever transmitted across the internet.
  • High-Resolution Preservation: Our engine preserves your image's full native resolution without degrading sharpness or adding unwanted compression artifacts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server to add watermarks?

No. Everything is rendered locally inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos never leave your device.

Can I tile the watermark repeatedly across the entire photo?

Yes! Simply select "Tile (Repeat)" in the Position dropdown to protect your entire image from unauthorized cropping or editing.

What image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and GIF images, and download your watermarked result as JPEG, PNG, or WebP.

Why should I watermark my images before posting online?

Watermarks protect against image theft, establish copyright ownership, and drive organic brand discovery when your visuals are shared across social platforms.
