How to Convert Pictures to a Single PDF Document Online

Submitting scanned ID cards, expense receipts, assignment worksheets, or a photo portfolio often requires submitting a single clean PDF rather than a dozen scattered image attachments. EasyToolz Images to PDF combines all your pictures into an orderly, formatted PDF in seconds.

  1. Select your photos: Select all the JPG, PNG, or WebP images you want to combine. You can drag and drop multiple files at once.
  2. Choose layout: Choose standard A4 or US Letter page size, and pick Portrait or Landscape orientation. Each image is automatically scaled to fit neatly within the margins without distortion.
  3. Generate PDF: Click "Generate & Download PDF" to instantly save your combined document to your computer or smartphone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many images can I combine into one PDF?

You can add as many photos as your device's memory supports. Most computers and smartphones easily handle 20-50 photos in one go.

Can I adjust the page size and orientation?

Yes! Choose between standard A4, US Letter, or A3 page formats, in either Portrait or Landscape orientation.

Are my photos uploaded to any third-party server?

Never. The PDF is constructed purely in client-side JavaScript using jsPDF. Your personal photos never touch any server.

Can I rearrange or delete photos before generating the PDF?

Yes! Each photo preview card has a delete button (✕) so you can review and organize before downloading.
