Permanent & Compliant PDF Redaction Online

Sharing sensitive files often requires blacking out Social Security Numbers (SSNs), credit card details, patient health identifiers (HIPAA), or trade secret figures. However, simply using a black highlighter in standard PDF viewers creates a false sense of security—anyone can copy and paste the underlying vector text layer.

Why True In-Browser Redaction Protects You

  • Irreversible Blackout: Our redaction engine burns permanent opaque rectangular pixels into the document's rendering stream. The underlying text is completely destroyed and cannot be highlighted or extracted.
  • Legal & HIPAA Compliance: Meets strict court-filing confidentiality rules and regulatory data-minimization mandates.
  • Total Privacy Guarantee: The redaction process executes 100% locally in your web browser memory. Your unredacted sensitive files are never uploaded to any remote server.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can anyone recover the text under the black boxes?

No. When you click "Apply & Download Redacted PDF", our engine rasterizes and bakes the solid black rectangles permanently into the page stream. The text cannot be recovered.

No. EasyToolz executes redaction entirely inside your local browser memory. Your documents never touch our servers.

Can I redact multiple pages in the same PDF?

Yes. Use the Previous and Next buttons to navigate through all pages, drawing redaction boxes on any page before downloading.
