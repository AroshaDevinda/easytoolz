Why Convert Documents to PDF/A for Long-Term Archiving?

Standard PDF files can become unreadable over time due to external font dependencies, deprecated encryption algorithms, dynamic scripting, or platform-specific color profiles. PDF/A (ISO 19005) is the international electronic document standard designed specifically for long-term digital preservation, ensuring your records open identically decades from now.

Mandatory Requirements for PDF/A Compliance

  • Font Independence: All glyphs, typography, and font descriptors must be permanently embedded without relying on system-installed fonts.
  • Device-Independent Color: Standards-compliant color profiles (such as sRGB IEC61966-2.1) are required via OutputIntents.
  • Ban on Active Code: Executable JavaScript, embedded audio/video clips, and external URL dependencies are disabled to prevent security decay.
  • Long-Term Standards: Mandated by the European Commission, US Federal Courts (CM/ECF), and international academic repositories.
