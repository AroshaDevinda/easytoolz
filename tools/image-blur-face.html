How to Blur Faces and Censor Photos Online Free

Sharing pictures on Reddit, social media, Craigslist, or company portals often requires hiding identities or concealing confidential numbers. EasyToolz lets you censor any photo with precision in seconds.

  1. Add your photo: Drag and drop your image file into the workspace or click to browse.
  2. Select the area to censor: Click and drag your mouse or finger across the face, license plate, house number, or confidential text snippet.
  3. Apply blur: Click "Apply Blur to Selection". You can adjust the blur intensity slider to make it mild or completely obscure the content. Repeat for additional areas as needed.
  4. Save your photo: Click "Download Censored Photo" to get your privacy-protected image instantly.

Common Scenarios for Image Blurring & Redaction

  • Protecting Children's Privacy: Obscure kids' faces before sharing event or school photos online.
  • Automotive Listings: Blur license plates and vehicle identification numbers (VINs) when selling cars online.
  • Document Redaction: Blur credit card CVV codes, social security numbers, medical records, or home addresses before sharing screenshots.
  • Bystander Anonymity: Respect the privacy of background bystanders in street photography or public protest imagery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my confidential photo safe when using this blur tool?

Yes, completely safe! The tool operates 100% in your browser memory using HTML5 Canvas. Your sensitive images, IDs, or family photos are never sent across the internet.

Can I blur multiple faces or areas on the same image?

Yes. Just drag a rectangle over the first face, click "Apply Blur to Selection", and repeat for as many faces, license plates, or names as you need.

Can someone un-blur or reverse the blurred photo?

No. The Gaussian blur algorithm permanently recomputes and averages the pixel values inside the selected box. Once downloaded, the original details cannot be reconstructed.

Can I undo a blur if I make a mistake?

Yes, click the "Undo Last" button to step back through each applied blur before downloading.
