How to Count Words Online

Whether you're writing a college essay with a strict word limit, composing a tweet within 280 characters, drafting a blog post, or preparing content for SEO — knowing your exact word count is essential. Our free Word Counter gives you real-time statistics as you type or paste your text.

  1. Paste or type your text: Enter your content in the text area above. The statistics update automatically as you type — no button needed.
  2. Review your statistics: See words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, and estimated reading time at a glance.
  3. Copy or clear: Use the buttons below the text area to copy your text to clipboard or clear the field.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter work?

Our word counter splits your text by whitespace to count words, counts all characters including spaces, detects sentences by punctuation marks (.!?), and identifies paragraphs by double line breaks.

What is the reading time based on?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute (WPM), which is the average adult silent reading speed. Speaking time would be slower at about 130 WPM.

Does it count special characters?

Yes, the character count includes all characters — letters, numbers, punctuation, and spaces. The "No Spaces" metric shows the count excluding spaces, useful for platforms with character limits.

Is my text stored or sent anywhere?

No. Everything happens locally in your browser. Your text is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server.
