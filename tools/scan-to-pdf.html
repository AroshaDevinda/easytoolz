Turn Any Device Camera into a Fast Document Scanner

Bulky flatbed scanners are no longer necessary for turning contracts, school assignments, invoices, and physical receipts into clean digital files. Our free Scan to PDF utility lets you snap pictures of pages in sequence and combines them into an organized, publication-ready PDF document right in your web browser.

Tips for Clean, High-Quality Document Scans

  1. Use Bright, Uniform Lighting: Position yourself near natural window light or under bright overhead lamps to eliminate shadows caused by your hand or mobile phone.
  2. Contrasting Background: Place dark papers on light desk surfaces, and light sheets against darker surfaces to make edge alignment clean.
  3. Hold the Camera Parallel: Keep your lens flat above the document rather than tilting at an angle to prevent geometric distortion.
  4. Flatten Creases: Smooth down paper wrinkles or folded receipts before capturing to keep typography crisp and readable.

Total Client-Side Privacy Protection

Traditional scanning apps frequently upload captured images to cloud servers for remote rendering, posing severe data leak risks for confidential IDs, banking statements, and legal agreements. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Yes. Capture or upload as many pages as needed. EasyToolz compiles them into a single, multi-page PDF document in exact sequential order.

No. EasyToolz works natively inside any modern browser on Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Windows with zero installations.

What formats can I upload if my camera is unavailable?

No. EasyToolz operates 100% client-side. No images, scans, or generated PDFs are transmitted or saved on remote servers.
