How to Extract Text from PDF Files Online for Free

Copying text manually from locked, formatted, or multi-page PDF documents can be tedious. EasyToolz PDF Text Extractor automatically extracts all text streams across every page, formats them with page dividers, and generates instant word and character counts.

  1. Add your PDF: Drag and drop your document into the box above or browse files from your device.
  2. Automatic text extraction: In milliseconds, the tool parses the document structure and displays the raw text in a clean editor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I extract text from a PDF document?

Drop your PDF into the upload zone above. The tool automatically scans all pages, extracts every text layer, provides word and character statistics, and lets you copy or download as .txt.

What if my PDF is a scanned photo or paper document?

If your PDF consists of scanned camera photos without a digital text layer, use our free OCR PDF tool to recognize the characters optically.

Are my PDF documents uploaded to a remote server?

Never. The extraction operates 100% locally via Mozilla's PDF.js library. Your confidential documents never leave your computer or mobile device.

Can I download the extracted text as a plain text file?

Yes! Click the "Download .txt" button to save the entire extracted text document with page demarcations.
