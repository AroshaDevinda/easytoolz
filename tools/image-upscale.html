How to Upscale Low Resolution Photos for Free

Have a small, grainy photo, avatar, or graphic that looks blurry when printed or displayed on modern high-DPI screens? Our browser-based Image Upscaler lets you blow up images up to 400% (4×) using multi-pass high-quality canvas interpolation.

  1. Select your photo: Drag and drop your image into the drop box above or click to select from your phone, tablet, or PC.
  2. Choose your multiplier: Select 2× (double dimensions), 3×, 4× (quadruple dimensions), or 1.5× depending on how large you need the output.
  3. Pick format & upscale: Choose PNG for crisp lines or JPEG for smaller file sizes, then click "Upscale & Download" to get your enlarged photo in seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this image upscaler enlarge images without losing quality?

Our tool uses stepped interpolation. Instead of blowing up an image in one harsh jump, it applies multiple smoothing passes with bilinear filtering to avoid blocky pixelation and retain sharp edges.

Is there a file size limit or daily quota?

No! Because processing happens in your device's browser memory, you can upscale as many photos as you want with zero limits.

Are my uploaded pictures stored on any server?

Never. All image processing is 100% client-side. Your photos stay strictly on your computer or mobile device.

Can I save the upscaled image as PNG, JPEG, or WebP?

Yes, you can choose between lossless PNG (best quality), WebP (modern web format), or JPEG (smaller file size).
