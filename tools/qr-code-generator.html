How to Create a QR Code Online

QR codes (Quick Response codes) are two-dimensional barcodes that can be scanned by any smartphone camera. They're used everywhere — on restaurant menus, business cards, event tickets, product packaging, and marketing materials. Our free generator lets you create them in seconds.

  1. Enter your content: Type or paste a URL, text message, phone number, or any data you want to encode.
  2. Customize (optional): Choose the QR code size and color to match your brand or design needs.
  3. Generate: Click the button to create your QR code instantly.
  4. Download: Save the QR code as a PNG image file to use in print materials, websites, or presentations.

Popular QR Code Use Cases

  • Website Links: Encode your website URL for business cards, flyers, or posters so people can visit with a scan.
  • Wi-Fi Sharing: Create a QR code for your Wi-Fi password so guests can connect without typing it.
  • Restaurant Menus: Replace paper menus with scannable QR codes on each table.
  • Event Tickets: Generate unique QR codes for each attendee's digital ticket.
  • App Downloads: Link directly to your app on the App Store or Google Play.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I encode in a QR code?

You can encode website URLs, plain text, phone numbers, email addresses, Wi-Fi credentials, and more. The most common use is encoding a URL so people can scan and open a website instantly.

Are the QR codes free to use commercially?

Yes! All QR codes generated here are 100% free for personal and commercial use. No watermarks, no restrictions, no hidden fees.

Do QR codes expire?

Static QR codes (like ours) never expire. The data is encoded directly in the code pattern, so it works forever — as long as the URL or content you linked remains active.

What size should I print my QR code?

For reliable scanning, print your QR code at least 2cm × 2cm (about 0.8 inches). For scanning from a distance (like a poster), use at least 10cm × 10cm. Our 300×300px or 500×500px options work well for most uses.
