How to Crop Images Online for Free

Need to crop a photo for a social media profile, remove unwanted edges from a screenshot, or resize an image to a specific aspect ratio? Our free Image Cropper lets you do it visually — just drag to select the area you want to keep.

  1. Upload your image: Drag and drop or click to select any JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF file.
  2. Select an aspect ratio (optional): Choose Free, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, or 3:4 for precise proportions.
  3. Draw your crop selection: Click and drag on the image to draw the crop area. The dimensions show in real-time.
  4. Crop and download: Choose your output format (PNG, JPEG, WebP) and click Crop & Download.

Why Use Our Browser-Based Cropper?

  • 100% Private: Your images never leave your device. We process everything locally in your browser.
  • Aspect Ratio Presets: Quickly crop to standard ratios like 1:1 for Instagram, 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails, or 4:3 for presentations.
  • Multiple Output Formats: Save as PNG (lossless), JPEG (smaller), or WebP (modern & efficient).
  • No Limits: Crop as many images as you want — no watermarks, no daily limits, no accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I crop images to specific aspect ratios?

Yes! Select from Free, 1:1 (square for Instagram), 4:3 (presentations), 16:9 (widescreen/YouTube), or 3:4 (portrait).

What formats can I save cropped images in?

PNG (lossless, transparency), JPEG (compressed, smaller), or WebP (modern, excellent quality at small sizes).

Is there a file size limit?

No server-side limit. Very large images (50MB+) may be slow depending on your device's memory.
