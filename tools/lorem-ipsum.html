Why Designers and Developers Use Lorem Ipsum Dummy Text

When presenting a website prototype, mobile application wireframe, or print brochure, readable text inevitably sidetracks clients into copyediting grammar rather than critiquing visual aesthetics, typography, balance, and spatial flow.

  1. Natural Letter Distribution: Unlike repeating "Content here, content here", classic Latin dummy text features realistic word lengths and paragraph cadences.
  2. Focus on Layout Architecture: Stakeholders evaluate font pairing, column widths, line heights, and margins without getting lost in narrative details.
  3. Flexible Output Sizes: Generate everything from single-line button microcopy to full 10-paragraph longform editorial articles.

The History of Lorem Ipsum

The passage dates back more than 2,000 years to Cicero's philosophical work De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum (On the Extremes of Good and Evil), composed in 45 BC.
