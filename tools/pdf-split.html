How to Split and Extract Pages from PDF Documents Free

Whether you need to pull an important chapter from an eBook, separate invoices from a monthly statement batch, or extract just the signature page from a legal contract, EasyToolz PDF Split makes it effortless and confidential.

  1. Upload your document: Drag and drop your PDF into the tool area or select it from your device.
  2. Choose how to split: Select "Custom page ranges" to isolate specific chapters (e.g., 1-5, 8, 12-15), or choose "Each page as separate PDF" to extract all pages individually.
  3. Download your split files: Click "Split & Download PDF". The extracted documents will download directly to your computer or phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I split specific page ranges from a PDF?

Select "Custom page ranges" and enter numbers separated by commas and hyphens, like "1-3, 5, 8-10". Each range will be generated as a separate PDF.

Are my sensitive documents uploaded to any server?

Never. All PDF splitting is executed locally in your browser using PDF-lib WebAssembly. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

Can I split a large multi-page PDF into single-page files?

Yes! Choose "Each page as separate PDF" and the tool will automatically generate and download individual PDFs for every page.

Will splitting a PDF reduce document quality?

Not at all. Text fonts, vector diagrams, and high-resolution images are transferred byte-for-byte with zero loss in quality.
