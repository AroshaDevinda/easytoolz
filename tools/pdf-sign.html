How to Sign PDF Documents Online Free Without Printing

Printing contracts just to sign them with a pen and scan them back in wastes paper, ink, and valuable time. EasyToolz Sign PDF lets you draw a clean electronic signature on your desktop, tablet, or phone, and seamlessly stamp it onto any page.

  1. Add your document: Upload your PDF file using the drop zone above or select it from your device.
  2. Draw your signature: Use your mouse, finger, or stylus to draw your natural signature on the virtual signature pad. Choose your preferred ink color and pen stroke width.
  3. Configure placement: Select which page number to sign (e.g. page 1 or the final signature page) and adjust alignment.
  4. Download signed contract: Click "Embed Signature & Download PDF" to retrieve your finalized, legally presentable PDF immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is signing a PDF with EasyToolz legally binding?

In many jurisdictions (including under the US ESIGN Act and European eIDAS), electronic signatures are legally valid for general agreements, employment forms, and service contracts.

Does EasyToolz upload my contract or signature to any server?

Never. All signature drawing and PDF embedding is rendered purely within your browser memory. Your confidential contracts and signature strokes never touch any server.

Can I sign PDFs using my finger or stylus on my phone or tablet?

Yes! The signature pad is fully optimized for touchscreens on iPhones, iPads, Android smartphones, and Microsoft Surface styluses.

Can I choose which page to place my signature on?

Yes. You can specify the exact page number and position (bottom-right, bottom-left, center, etc.) and scale the signature size.
