Popular Text Case Formats Explained

  • Title Case: Capitalizes major words (e.g. The Quick Brown Fox). Essential for article headings, book titles, and YouTube video captions.
  • Sentence Case: Capitalizes the initial letter of sentences and leaves the rest lowercase (e.g. The quick brown fox jumps.). Perfect for standard paragraphs and prose.
  • camelCase & PascalCase: Joins words together without spaces, capitalizing internal words (e.g. myVariableName or ClassName). Standard in programming languages.
  • snake_case & kebab-case: Separates words using underscores (user_profile_id) or hyphens (user-profile-id) for URL slugs and CSS classnames.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each significant word (ideal for article headlines and book titles). Sentence case capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence and proper nouns.

What are camelCase and snake_case used for?

These are naming conventions used in software development. camelCase (firstWordLowerCase) is common in JavaScript and Java, while snake_case (words_joined_by_underscores) is standard in Python and database columns.
