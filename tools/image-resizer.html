How to Resize Images Online for Free

Whether you need to meet the strict image dimension requirements for a passport photo, resize a banner for your blog, or scale down a massive 4K photograph for an email attachment, our free image resizer makes it effortless.

  1. Select your image: Drag and drop your JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF image into the area above, or tap the box to choose a file from your device.
  2. Enter custom dimensions: Type the exact Width and Height in pixels that you need. Alternatively, click one of our quick presets (like 1920×1080 FHD) to instantly populate the dimensions.
  3. Lock the aspect ratio (Optional): Keep the "Maintain Aspect Ratio" toggle enabled to ensure your image doesn't look stretched or squished when you change one dimension.
  4. Resize and Download: Click the primary button to instantly resize your image and trigger the download to your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Maintain Aspect Ratio" mean?

Aspect ratio is the proportional relationship between the width and height of an image. If you maintain it, changing the width will automatically adjust the height so that your image doesn't look distorted.

Will resizing an image reduce its file size?

Yes, reducing the physical dimensions (width and height) of an image significantly reduces the total number of pixels, which in turn results in a smaller file size in megabytes (MB) or kilobytes (KB).

Can I enlarge a small image?

While you can enter dimensions larger than your original image, doing so is called "upscaling." Upscaling typically results in a blurry or pixelated image because the browser has to invent new pixels to fill the larger space.
