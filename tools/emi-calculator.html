How to Calculate Your Loan EMI

Planning to take a home loan, car loan, or personal loan? Understanding your Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) before borrowing helps you budget effectively and avoid financial strain. Our free EMI Calculator gives you instant results without needing to visit a bank.

  1. Enter the loan amount: Type the total amount you plan to borrow.
  2. Set the interest rate: Enter the annual interest rate offered by your bank (e.g., 8.5%).
  3. Choose the tenure: Select how many years you want to repay the loan over.
  4. Click Calculate: Instantly see your monthly EMI, total interest payable, and total repayment amount.

Understanding the EMI Formula

EMI is calculated using the standard reducing balance formula:

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)N] / [(1+R)N - 1]
  • P = Principal loan amount
  • R = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • N = Total number of monthly installments (years × 12)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay to your bank or lender every month to repay your loan. Each EMI payment includes both principal repayment and interest charges.

Does a higher tenure reduce EMI?

Yes, a longer loan tenure reduces your monthly EMI. However, you end up paying significantly more total interest over the life of the loan. Our calculator shows both values so you can compare scenarios.

Can I use this for home loans and car loans?

Absolutely. This calculator works for any type of loan — home loans, car loans, personal loans, education loans, or business loans. Just enter the principal, interest rate, and tenure.

How can I reduce my EMI?

You can reduce your EMI by: (1) negotiating a lower interest rate, (2) extending the loan tenure, (3) making a larger down payment to reduce the principal, or (4) making part-prepayments when you have surplus funds.
