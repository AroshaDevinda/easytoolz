Frequently Asked Questions

Which format should I choose?

JPEG is best for photographs and images with complex colors. PNG is best when you need a transparent background or sharp lines (like logos or text). WebP is a modern format developed by Google that provides excellent quality at very small file sizes, making it perfect for websites.

Why does my PNG lose transparency when converted to JPEG?

The JPEG format does not support transparency (alpha channels). When you convert a PNG with a transparent background to a JPEG, the transparent areas will automatically be filled in with a solid color, typically black or white depending on the browser.
