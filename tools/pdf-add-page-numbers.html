How to Add Page Numbers to PDF Documents Free Online

Academic essays, legal exhibits, business reports, and book manuscripts require consistent pagination for easy reference. EasyToolz Add Page Numbers lets you paginate documents cleanly with complete control over format and positioning.

  1. Select your PDF: Drag and drop your document into the upload zone above or browse files on your device.
  2. Configure pagination: Choose header or footer placement (e.g. Bottom Center or Top Right) and choose your style (1, 2, 3 or Page 1 of N).
  3. Exclude cover pages: Set "Start Numbering From Page" to 2 if your first page is a cover title page.
  4. Download your numbered PDF: Click "Add Page Numbers & Download PDF" to instantly save your numbered file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I skip numbering the first page or cover page?

Yes! Simply change "Start from page" to 2 (or any page number) to leave the cover page clean and unnumbered.

What page numbering styles are available?

You can choose standard numbers (1, 2, 3), formal pagination ("Page 1 of N"), or decorative dashes ("— 1 —").

Where can I position the page numbers?

You can place numbers at Bottom Center, Bottom Right, Bottom Left, Top Center, or Top Right.

Are my PDF documents uploaded to a remote server?

Never. All pagination is rendered entirely inside your browser memory. Your confidential documents stay 100% on your machine.
