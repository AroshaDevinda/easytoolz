Age Calculator

Calculate your exact age in years, months and days — instantly

How to Calculate Your Age Online

Whether you need your exact age for a government form, a job application, or you're simply curious about how many days you've been alive, our free Age Calculator gives you an instant, precise answer — no sign-up or app download required.

  1. Enter your date of birth: Select the day, month, and year you were born using the date picker above.
  2. Choose the target date (optional): By default, the calculator uses today's date. You can change this to any date — for example, to find out how old you were on a specific historical event.
  3. Click "Calculate Age": Instantly see your exact age broken down into years, months, and days, plus total days lived and a countdown to your next birthday.

Why Use Our Free Online Age Calculator?

Calculating age sounds simple, but getting the exact number of months and days right — especially across leap years and varying month lengths — is surprisingly tricky. Our tool handles all of that automatically.

  • Exact Results: Unlike rough "subtract the year" calculations, our tool gives you precise years, months, and days accounting for leap years and different month lengths.
  • Total Days Lived: See the grand total of days you've experienced — a fun fact that's also useful for medical and legal calculations.
  • Next Birthday Countdown: Find out exactly how many days until your next birthday celebration.
  • 100% Private: Your date of birth is never sent to any server. Everything is calculated locally in your browser.
  • Works on Any Device: Use it on your phone, tablet, or desktop — no app installation needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Age Calculator work?

Our Age Calculator computes the difference between your date of birth and a target date, breaking it down into exact years, months, and days. It also calculates total days lived and your next birthday countdown.

Can I calculate age between any two dates?

Yes! By default, the tool uses today's date, but you can change the "Calculate Age On" field to any date you like — past or future — to find the exact duration between any two dates.

Is this Age Calculator accurate for leap years?

Yes. The calculator uses JavaScript's native Date object which correctly handles leap years, so your result is always precise down to the day.

Do you store my date of birth?

No. Your date of birth never leaves your device. The calculation happens entirely in your web browser — we don't have servers that process or store any personal data.
