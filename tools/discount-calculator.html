The Math Behind Discount Calculations

The standard formula for calculating sale prices is straightforward:

Savings = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)
Final Price = Original Price - Savings

To reverse-calculate the regular price when you only know the discounted sale price and the discount percentage:

Original Price = Sale Price ÷ (1 - (Discount Percentage ÷ 100))

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate a percentage discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage divided by 100 to find the savings: Savings = Original Price × (Discount % / 100). Subtract the savings from the original price to get the final sale price.

How do I find the original price if I only know the sale price?

Use our built-in reverse calculator or the formula: Original Price = Sale Price / (1 - Discount % / 100).

What is a 20% discount on $80?

20% of $80 is $16. The final price after the discount is $80 - $16 = $64.

Can I calculate stacked or double discounts?

If an item has an additional clearance discount (e.g. 20% off plus an extra 10% off), calculate the first 20% off the original price, and then apply the 10% off the reduced price.
