Convert Excel Spreadsheets to Professional PDFs

Sharing native Excel files with clients or stakeholders can lead to accidental formula tampering, formatting distortions across different software versions, and security risks. Converting your workbooks to PDF (Portable Document Format) preserves cell alignments, tables, and financial layouts identically on any screen or printer.

Why Use In-Browser Spreadsheet Conversion?

  • Protect Proprietary Formulas: PDFs lock in calculated outputs without revealing proprietary business logic or raw source formulas.
  • Landscape Optimization: Wide tables with dozens of columns are automatically oriented to fit standard landscape pages cleanly.
  • Complete Confidentiality: Spreadsheets frequently hold sensitive accounting ledgers, employee salaries, and customer lists. EasyToolz executes 100% in your browser memory so your corporate numbers are never transmitted over the internet.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will my spreadsheets stay private?

Yes. EasyToolz uses SheetJS running locally inside your browser sandbox. No workbook data or table rows are sent to any remote server.

How do I make sure wide columns are not cut off?

Select "Landscape" in the Orientation dropdown. This doubles the printable horizontal width for multi-column financial statements.

Can I convert CSV files exported from databases?

Yes. Comma-separated (.csv) and tab-delimited (.tsv) files are fully supported alongside standard Excel workbooks.

Is there any fee or file size limit?

No. EasyToolz is 100% free with unlimited conversions and zero account sign-up.
