    Effective Task Management Methodologies

    • The 1-3-5 Rule: Plan each day around 1 major task, 3 medium tasks, and 5 minor tasks. This prevents overwhelming checklists and ensures meaningful progress.
    • Eisenhower Matrix: Categorize items by urgency and importance. High-priority tasks receive your freshest morning focus before reactive communications take over.
    • Pomodoro Technique: Pair your checklist items with 25-minute focused work sprints separated by 5-minute restorative pauses.

    Complete Data Privacy with In-Browser Storage

    Unlike third-party task platforms that harvest user behavioral data, EasyToolz stores all items in HTML5 localStorage on your local device. Your sensitive to-do items, work projects, and personal reminders remain strictly between you and your computer screen.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Where are my to-do tasks stored?

    All checklist entries are saved in your browser's localStorage. No tasks are sent across the internet, protecting your confidential notes and privacy.

    Will my tasks stay if I restart my computer?

    Yes. Browser localStorage persists through system restarts and browser closures. Tasks remain available until you explicitly clear your browser storage.

    Can I export my task list to a text file?

    Yes. Simply click the "Export" button above to download your complete to-do checklist as a readable plain text file.
